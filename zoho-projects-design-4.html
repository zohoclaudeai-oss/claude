PLATFORM OVERVIEW

What Is Zoho Projects

Zoho Projects is a cloud-native project management platform built for organizations that need more than task lists. It provides Work Breakdown Structures, Gantt charts with Critical Path rendering, stage-gate Blueprint automation, timesheet-driven billing, resource allocation heatmaps, and cross-project portfolio dashboards. Unlike lightweight tools, Zoho Projects connects natively to the entire Zoho ecosystem -- CRM, Books, Invoice, People, Analytics -- creating a single operating layer from pipeline to cash. Elite Tech Corp implements it as a governed PMO operating system, not just a tool deployment.

THE PROBLEM

Symptoms of a Broken PMO

Scattered Spreadsheets

Project data lives across dozens of Excel files, Google Sheets, and email threads with no single source of truth.

Zero Visibility

Leadership cannot see real-time project status, resource utilization, or budget burn without requesting manual reports.

Missed Deadlines

Without critical path tracking or dependency management, delays cascade silently across the portfolio.

Budget Overruns

No real-time budget vs. actual tracking means financial surprises surface only at project closure.

Timesheet Chaos

Manual time tracking across tools makes billing inaccurate and resource planning unreliable.

Process Inconsistency

Every PM runs projects differently with no standardized stage-gates, templates, or approval workflows.

Disconnected Tools

CRM, finance, HR, and project management operate as isolated islands requiring manual data transfer.

No Forecasting

Resource demand, capacity gaps, and revenue projections are guesswork without unified project data.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Answer

What is Zoho Projects and who is it for?+

Zoho Projects is a cloud-based project management platform designed for organizations of all sizes. It offers task management, Gantt charts, timesheets, Blueprint workflows, and resource management. It is ideal for PMOs, delivery teams, and organizations needing structured project governance.

How long does implementation take?+

Implementation timelines range from 7 working days for Basic setups to 45 working days for Elite enterprise deployments. The exact duration depends on scope, integrations, data migration requirements, and organizational complexity.

Can Zoho Projects integrate with our existing CRM?+

Yes. Zoho Projects integrates natively with Zoho CRM, enabling automatic project creation from won deals, bi-directional data sync, and unified customer-project views. Integration with third-party CRMs is also possible via APIs and Zoho Flow.

Do you provide training for our team?+

Yes. Every package includes hands-on training sessions ranging from 2 hours (Basic) to 12 hours (Elite). Training covers platform usage, workflow management, reporting, and admin functions tailored to each user role.

What happens after go-live?+

Our Elite package includes 30 days of post-go-live support. All packages include documentation and knowledge transfer. Extended support and managed services contracts are available for ongoing optimization.

Can you migrate data from our existing tools?+

Yes. We handle data migration from spreadsheets, MS Project, Jira, Asana, Trello, and other project management tools. Professional and Elite packages include migration assistance with data validation and integrity checks.

Is Zoho Projects suitable for Agile teams?+

Absolutely. Zoho Projects supports sprint boards, backlog management, and velocity tracking alongside traditional Gantt and waterfall views, making it ideal for hybrid Agile-waterfall environments.

What industries do you specialize in?+

We serve 15+ verticals including technology, financial services, construction, healthcare, manufacturing, retail, logistics, and government. Our implementation approach is tailored to each industry's specific project governance needs.

How does timesheet billing work?+

Team members log time against tasks. Managers approve timesheets through a defined workflow. Approved hours flow to Zoho Books or Invoice with cost-rate calculations, enabling automated client billing with full audit trails.

What is the pricing model?+

Our implementation is a one-time project fee based on the package selected. Zoho Projects subscription fees are billed separately by Zoho on a per-user basis. Detailed pricing is available on request during the strategy call.
