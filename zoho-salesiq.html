1

Landing

A prospect visits the franchise opportunity page, or the bot proactively reaches out based on browsing behaviour.

2

Opening question

The bot asks which city or region they’re considering.

3

Live availability check

The requested territory is checked instantly against the live map inside the chat.

4

Branch point

If available, the visitor gets immediate confirmation; if not, nearby open territories are suggested instead.

5

Qualification

Investment capacity band, timeline, and prior business experience are captured through natural conversation.

6

Real answers given

Questions about investment range, included support, and typical launch timeline are answered directly, not deferred.

Intent scoring

The conversation’s overall quality sets a lead priority score automatically.

8

Branch point

A high scoring prospect during business hours is offered immediate live handover; others are queued for standard follow up.

9

Lead creation

The full transcript and qualification data are attached to a new CRM record either way.
