Key Features of Zoho Projects

Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)

What It Does
Decomposes projects into hierarchical phases, milestones, task lists, and tasks — giving every deliverable a traceable place in the plan.
How It Works
Create parent-child task hierarchies up to 5 levels deep, apply templates for repeatable project types, and auto-roll-up progress to milestone and project levels.

Gantt Charts

What It Does
Visualises the entire project schedule with task bars, dependency arrows, milestones, and baseline comparisons on a zoomable timeline.
How It Works
Drag-and-drop task bars to reschedule, link tasks with FS/FF/SS/SF dependencies, toggle baseline overlay to spot schedule variance, and export to PDF for stakeholders.

Critical Path Method (CPM)

What It Does
Identifies the longest chain of dependent tasks that determines the earliest possible project completion date — any slip on this chain delays the whole project.
How It Works
Toggle CPM view in the Gantt chart; critical tasks highlight in red. Adjust durations or add resources to shorten the critical path and recover schedule.

Baselines & Variance Tracking

What It Does
Freezes an approved project schedule as a baseline so every future change is measured against the original commitment.
How It Works
Set a baseline at project kick-off, then overlay it on the Gantt chart. Variance columns show days ahead/behind for every task, milestone, and phase.

Resource Capacity Planning

What It Does
Shows who is available, who is overloaded, and who has bandwidth — across all projects in real time.
How It Works
Capacity heatmaps display utilization percentages per person per week. Drag tasks between resources to level-load the team and prevent burnout.

Time Tracking & Timesheets

What It Does
Captures actual hours worked per task, per person, per day — feeding project costing, billing, and payroll downstream.
How It Works
Team members log time via timer, manual entry, or mobile app. Managers approve timesheets weekly. Approved hours flow to Zoho Books for invoicing and Zoho People for payroll.

Billing Rates & Project Costing

What It Does
Assigns per-user or per-role billing rates so actual project costs and revenue are calculated automatically from approved timesheets.
How It Works
Configure rates at project or global level. The system multiplies approved hours by rates to produce cost vs. revenue reports and gross margin dashboards.

Timesheet Approval Workflows

What It Does
Routes submitted timesheets through configurable approval chains before they count toward billing or payroll.
How It Works
Set approval hierarchies by project, department, or role. Approvers receive notifications, review entries, approve or reject with comments, and approved timesheets auto-sync to Books.

Stage-Gate Blueprints

What It Does
Enforces mandatory process gates — approvals, checklists, document uploads — that must be cleared before a task or project can advance to the next stage.
How It Works
Design visual state-transition diagrams with drag-and-drop. Assign gate owners, mandatory fields, SLA timers, and escalation rules. Non-compliant tasks cannot bypass the gate.

Project Budgeting & EVM

What It Does
Tracks planned vs. actual costs at task and project level, with Earned Value Management metrics (CPI, SPI, EAC, ETC) for objective performance measurement.
How It Works
Set budgets by phase or cost category. The system calculates Earned Value from % complete and actual costs, surfacing CPI and SPI variances before margins erode.

Client Portals

What It Does
Gives external clients a controlled view of their project — milestones, tasks, documents, and discussions — without exposing internal data.
How It Works
Enable portal access per project. Clients see only what you share: milestone progress, approved documents, and discussion threads. They can comment, upload files, and approve deliverables.

Subcontractor Access & Controls

What It Does
Grants external vendors and subcontractors task-level access with role-based permissions so they deliver within your governance framework.
How It Works
Invite vendors as external users with scoped access. They see only assigned tasks, log time against them, and upload deliverables. Blueprints enforce quality gates on their outputs.

Issue Tracking

What It Does
Captures bugs, defects, and issues with severity, priority, and assignee — linked to the tasks and milestones they impact.
How It Works
Log issues manually or via email/portal. Classify by severity and priority, assign to owners, track resolution through Blueprint workflows, and link to parent tasks for traceability.

Custom Modules & Fields

What It Does
Extends Zoho Projects with custom data modules — equipment registers, vendor scorecards, risk logs — tailored to your industry.
How It Works
Create custom modules via Layout Rules or Zoho Creator. Add custom fields (text, lookup, formula) to tasks, issues, and timesheets. Related lists link modules for 360-degree views.

Wiki, Documents & Knowledge Base

What It Does
Centralises project documentation, SOPs, meeting notes, and knowledge articles — versioned, searchable, and linked to tasks.
How It Works
Create wiki pages with rich-text editor, attach files from Zoho WorkDrive or Google Drive, tag documents to milestones, and set access permissions per role.

Native CRM, Books & People Integration

What It Does
Connects project delivery to the commercial engine — deals flow into projects, timesheets flow into invoices, tasks flow into HR workload tracking.
How It Works
CRM deal-won triggers project creation. Books pulls approved timesheets for invoicing. People reflects project allocations in HR dashboards. All sync is real-time, bi-directional, and zero-code.

Deluge Scripting & Custom Functions

What It Does
Automates complex business logic — auto-assign tasks, escalate overdue items, calculate custom KPIs — without external tools.
How It Works
Write Deluge scripts triggered by task events, scheduled timers, or Blueprint transitions. Access any Zoho API, send notifications, create records, and update fields programmatically.

Zoho Analytics & BI Dashboards

What It Does
Delivers executive-grade BI dashboards that blend project, financial, HR, and CRM data into a single portfolio view.
How It Works
Pre-built connectors sync Projects data into Zoho Analytics. Build pivot tables, KPI widgets, and drill-down charts. Schedule reports to leadership inboxes and embed dashboards in client portals.
