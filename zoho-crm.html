Elite Tech CorpQuick flow · the journey

How Zoho CRM moves a
franchise prospect forward

The full user journey, then the exact workflow with every decision point, drawn from the Zoho Suite for Preschool Franchises specification.

1

Capture

A franchise enquiry enters the pipeline from any source: form, chat, social, expo, or referral.

2

Deduplication check

The system checks whether this prospect already exists; matches are flagged for manual review rather than auto merged.

3

Qualification scoring

Investment capacity, timeline, and background produce an automatic score.

4

Territory check

The requested city is checked for availability; if open, it’s provisionally reserved for the active discussion, and if not, demand is logged as a sales signal.

5

Discovery call

Logged against the record using a structured call template that captures the answers that matter.

6

Disclosure stage

The information kit and financial disclosure documents are issued and tracked to completion.

7

Site approval

Proposed premises are submitted with documents and photos, then reviewed against defined criteria.

8

Branch point

Approved premises move the deal to the agreement stage; rejected submissions return to the franchisee with specific required changes.

9

Agreement

The franchise agreement, territory addendum, and licensing terms are issued through Zoho Sign and tracked to signature.

10

Conversion

On signature, the lead automatically becomes a franchisee record: territory assigned, campus created, users provisioned, and royalty terms recorded in Books.
