Platform overview

What Is Zoho Projects

Zoho Projects is a cloud-based enterprise project management and operational governance platform that unifies project planning, resource scheduling, task execution, timesheet billing, issue tracking, and budget controls into a single-pane-of-glass workspace.

It replaces disconnected Excel tracking sheets, lost WhatsApp/Slack chat threads, untracked scope creep, and chaotic month-end billing reconciliations -- giving leadership real-time delivery visibility, predictable capacity, and clean cash flow.

Zoho Projects holds the entire deliverable lifecycle in one governed architecture: from CRM Deal Won to milestone billing and 1-click invoicing in Zoho Books.
