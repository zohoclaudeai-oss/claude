Elite Tech CorpQuick flow · the journey

How Zoho Social moves a
franchise prospect forward

The full user journey, then the exact workflow with every decision point, drawn from the Zoho Suite for Preschool Franchises specification.

01

Discovery

A prospect sees a territory availability post, franchisee success story, or expansion update on a brand or franchise opportunity channel.

02

Engagement

They like, share, or start reading more about the opportunity, often browsing several posts across a few days.

03

Expressed interest

They comment or send a direct message asking about investment amount, availability, or how to get started.

04

Automatic detection

The system scans the message for franchise intent signals and tags it as a franchise enquiry rather than general engagement.

05

Lead creation

A franchise lead record is created automatically in CRM, carrying the source post, channel, and territory context.

06

Notification

Franchise development receives an immediate alert with the enquiry and its background attached.

07

First outreach

The sales team makes contact, referencing the exact post or story that sparked the enquiry.

08

Nurture continuation

If the prospect isn’t ready to move forward immediately, they flow into a long term Zoho Campaigns sequence for ongoing engagement.
