Product Overview

What Is Zoho Payroll

Zoho Payroll is a cloud payroll application that calculates salaries, applies statutory deductions, generates payslips and Form 16, disburses pay and posts the journal to your accounts.

Zoho Payroll holds the entire pay cycle in one place, from employee master → salary structure → attendance input → statutory deduction → approval → payslip → direct deposit → accounting.

It replaces the Excel salary sheets, manual EPF and ESI workings and emailed payslips most finance teams still rely on, and gives you a pay run that reconciles the first time.

Who Needs Zoho Payroll

Small & Mid-sized Businesses

Run compliant payroll without hiring a dedicated payroll executive.

Growing Companies

Keep one pay process as headcount and salary structures multiply.

Shift-based Workforces

Attendance-linked pay, overtime and wage-rate driven salary runs.

Regulated Employers

EPF, ESI, PT and TDS with an audit trail you can produce on demand.

Employee Master → Salary Structure → Attendance → Deductions → Statutory → Approval → Payslip → Payment → Accounting

Zoho Payroll pulls attendance and leave from Zoho People, reimbursements from Zoho Expense and posts the payroll journal to Zoho Books.

Functional Capabilities

Key Features of Zoho Payroll

Comprehensive capability across all 19 functional areas of payroll, from the employee master to the full and final settlement.

1. Organisation Setup

What it does: Holds your company, locations and structure.

How it works: Company details, work locations, departments and designations configured once and reused.

Benefit: One payroll master for every location

2. Users, Roles & Permissions

What it does: Controls who can see and approve salary data.

How it works: Roles are defined by responsibility with view, create, edit and approve rights mapped per user.

Benefit: Salary data stays where it should

3. Pay Schedule

What it does: Defines when your payroll actually runs.

How it works: Pay frequency, pay periods, first payroll date and pay-day rules are set to your cycle.

Benefit: The calendar drives payroll, not chasing

4. Salary Components

What it does: Builds the earnings and deductions you actually use.

How it works: Basic, allowances, bonuses and deductions with formulas, percentages, limits and dependencies.

Benefit: Structures that mirror your offer letters

5. Salary Structures

What it does: Groups components into reusable templates.

How it works: Structures are created per employee category or grade and mapped to individual employees.

Benefit: New joiners configured in minutes

6. EPF, ESI & PT

What it does: Applies statutory deductions to every pay run.

How it works: Contribution rates, eligibility, wage limits and slabs are configured and validated.

Benefit: Compliance calculated, not remembered

7. Organisation Tax Setup

What it does: Registers your employer tax identity in the system.

How it works: Employer tax identification numbers, payroll tax rates and company tax settings.

Benefit: Filings that match your registrations

8. Employee Tax Declaration

What it does: Collects investment declarations through self service.

How it works: Employees declare planned tax-saving investments for the financial year themselves.

Benefit: No more declaration chasing by email

9. Proof Submission & Verification

What it does: Verifies proofs before the final tax computation.

How it works: Employees upload proofs and payroll admins verify them before tax is finalised.

Benefit: Defensible TDS, every year

10. Form 16 Generation

What it does: Produces and distributes Form 16 at year end.

How it works: Form 16 is generated automatically for every employee and published to self service.

Benefit: Year end without a scramble

11. Employee Payroll Data

What it does: Keeps every payroll-relevant record in one place.

How it works: Employee details, bank information, salary structures and EPF/ESI eligibility per person.

Benefit: One record, no side spreadsheets

12. Employee Self Service

What it does: Gives employees their own payroll screen.

How it works: Payslips, tax declarations and salary information available without asking payroll.

Benefit: Payroll stops answering the same query

13. Payroll Approval Workflow

What it does: Puts a review step before money moves.

How it works: Multi-level approval must complete before a pay run is finalised and released.

Benefit: Nothing pays out unreviewed

14. Variance & Exception Alerts

What it does: Flags anything unusual before payout.

How it works: Month-on-month salary variances and exceptions are surfaced for review.

Benefit: Catch the error before the credit

15. Direct Deposit

What it does: Disburses salary straight from the pay run.

How it works: Native bank integration is configured and validated for direct employee credit.

Benefit: One approval, everyone paid

16. Loans & Advances

What it does: Recovers company loans automatically.

How it works: Loan types, eligibility and EMI schedules deduct from salary each month on their own.

Benefit: Recoveries that never get forgotten

17. Payslip & PDF Templates

What it does: Formats payslips and statements to your brand.

How it works: Salary, email and PDF templates control the payslips and statutory statements employees receive.

Benefit: Documents that look like you sent them

18. Integrations

What it does: Connects payroll to HR, expenses and the books.

How it works: Zoho People, Books, Expense and Analytics, plus Tally, QuickBooks and biometric devices.

Benefit: Inputs in, journal out, no re-keying

19. Full & Final Settlement

What it does: Closes an exit properly and on time.

How it works: Leave encashment, pending reimbursements and notice-pay recovery compute into one statement.

Benefit: Clean exits, settled on schedule

Knowledge Base

Zoho Payroll FAQ

Straight answers to the implementation, compliance and integration questions finance teams ask us first.

1. What is Zoho Payroll?
Zoho Payroll is a cloud payroll application that calculates salaries against your structures, applies statutory deductions, runs approvals, disburses pay, publishes payslips and posts the payroll journal to your accounts.
2. How long does implementation take?
It depends on scope. Our Basic package goes live in 7 working days, Professional in 14 working days and Elite in 30 working days, counted from the day we receive your salary structures, employee data and statutory registration details.
3. Does it handle Indian statutory compliance?
Yes. The India edition covers EPF, ESI, professional tax and TDS, configured against your own registrations, contribution rates, wage limits and state slabs, with calculations validated before each pay run.
4. Can employees submit tax declarations themselves?
Yes. Employees declare planned investments and upload proofs through employee self service, and your payroll admins verify them before the final tax computation. Form 16 is then generated and published at year end.
5. Where does attendance and leave data come from?
From Zoho People natively, so approved attendance and leave flow into the pay run without a manual import. For organisations not using Zoho People, we sync biometric devices directly in the Elite package.
6. Can we pay salaries directly from Zoho Payroll?
Yes. In the Elite package we configure and validate native bank integration so approved pay runs disburse by direct deposit, with employee bank details verified beforehand in Professional.
7. Does it post to our accounts?
Yes. The payroll journal posts automatically to Zoho Books. For non-Zoho accounting, we configure journal export to Tally or QuickBooks in the Elite package.
8. What support do we get after go-live?
We stay through your first full cycle: the first pay run, the first statutory filing and the first month-end reconciliation, then move you to ongoing support for structure changes and enhancements.
