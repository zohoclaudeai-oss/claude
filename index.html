Elite Tech CorpThe eleven apps

Here's the short version of
what each app is actually for.

Ask most franchisors what their website does and they will say "generates leads." Ask which kind, and things get vague fast. That vagueness is expensive. It is how a serious franchise investor's enquiry ends up answered with the same templated reply as a parent asking about nap schedules.

One rule, eleven apps

We set up connected Zoho apps around a simple rule: the franchise pipeline is where your business actually grows, so it gets real depth. Qualification, territory checks, contracts, onboarding, years of ongoing relationship. The admission pipeline exists to do one thing well: recognize a parent enquiry, figure out which of your franchisees owns that territory, and get it there before someone else's school does.

AttractZoho Social

Your franchise opportunity content lives here, aimed at people who Google things like "how much does it cost to open a preschool." General brand posts too, but growth content is the priority.

NurtureZoho Campaigns

A long, patient nurture sequence for franchise prospects. This is a decision that takes months, not a Tuesday afternoon. Admission enquiries get one short "we've passed you along" message and nothing further.

CaptureZoho Forms

A serious intake form for franchise applicants (capital available, target city, relevant experience) sitting next to a deliberately short "find a campus" form that exists purely to route a parent correctly.

OptimiseZoho PageSense

Constant testing on your franchise opportunity page, because a single extra qualified applicant is worth more than a hundred extra admission enquiries you are not even fulfilling.

QualifyZoho SalesIQ

Chat that qualifies a franchise prospect properly, and a separate, much shorter flow that just asks a parent their city and hands them off.

ConvergeZoho CRM

Two pipelines living in one system, deliberately unequal in weight. Franchise: full Blueprint, from first enquiry to an operating campus. Admission: normalize, match territory, assign to the franchisee, close the loop.

AgreeZoho Sign

Franchise agreements and territory contracts, the documents that actually define your business relationships.

EarnZoho Books

Franchise fees and the royalty stream from every open campus. This is your revenue, tracked properly.

SupportZoho Desk

Franchisee questions about marketing assets, royalty statements, and operations. Parent complaints about pickup times are a conversation for the campus, not for you.

MeasureZoho Survey

How happy your franchisees are with the support and training you provide — arguably the single number that predicts whether your network keeps growing.

InsightZoho Analytics

Franchise pipeline health, territory availability, network-wide performance. Admission volume shows up as a number worth glancing at, not a dashboard worth building out.
